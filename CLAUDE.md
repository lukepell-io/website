## Copy rules for lukepell.io

- Every title on the site is in Title Case: page and section headings, card titles, eyebrow labels, FAQ questions, credit roles, job titles and skills. Keep short words lowercase unless they start the title: a, an, the, and, but, or, for, to, of, in, on, at, by.
- Buttons, links and body text stay in sentence case.
- British spelling. No em dashes in headings, buttons or nav.
- All wording lives in `content/site.ts` and `content/projects.ts`.
